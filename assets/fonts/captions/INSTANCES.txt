Fonts of the Remotion caption templates (remotion/templates/*.json).

Every font here is under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL-<Family>.txt) or the Apache License 2.0
(LICENSE-<Family>.txt), downloaded unmodified from https://github.com/google/fonts (folders ofl/<family>
and apache/<family>), with the exceptions below.

Static instances. The caption renderer draws glyph outlines read from the font file and needs one face
per file, so these five were cut from the upstream variable fonts with fontTools 4.68
(fontTools.varLib.instancer, overlaps kept and flagged). Nothing else was changed: same glyphs, same
names, same licence (OFL 1.1; none of these families declares a Reserved Font Name).

  TiltNeon-Regular.ttf              from ofl/tiltneon  TiltNeon[XROT,YROT].ttf            at XROT=0 YROT=0
  Fredoka-Bold.ttf                  from ofl/fredoka   Fredoka[wdth,wght].ttf             at wght=700 wdth=100
  Unbounded-Black.ttf               from ofl/unbounded Unbounded[wght].ttf                at wght=900
  JetBrainsMono-ExtraBold.ttf       from ofl/jetbrainsmono JetBrainsMono[wght].ttf        at wght=800
  BricolageGrotesque-ExtraBold.ttf  from ofl/bricolagegrotesque BricolageGrotesque[opsz,wdth,wght].ttf
                                                                                        at opsz=96 wght=800 wdth=100

Emoji are not here on purpose: captions use Apple Color Emoji, which ships with macOS and may not be
redistributed. Where it is not installed, emoji are left out of the caption text.

The fonts of the ASS caption styles (Anton, Bangers, Luckiest Guy) stay one folder up, where libass
looks for them; this folder is not scanned by libass.
